# Building Things for the Web

**Independent web projects · Word Games · Interactive Tools**

I enjoy exploring ideas and turning them into simple, interactive web experiences. My projects focus on browser-based games, word puzzles and practical tools designed to be easy to use and accessible to everyone.

I'm always experimenting with new concepts, improving existing projects and finding creative ways to make everyday web experiences more engaging.

---

### What I'm Working On

- 🎮 **Browser Games** – Creating and improving online games that are easy to access and fun to play.
- 🧩 **Word Puzzles & Solvers** – Exploring interactive tools that help players solve puzzles, discover words and improve their skills.
- 🛠️ **Web Tools** – Building practical utilities with a focus on simplicity, usability and performance.

### Featured Projects

**🧩 [PlayCrosswords.org](https://playcrosswords.org)**

A growing collection of online crossword games, word puzzles and interactive solving tools. The goal is to bring together entertaining challenges and useful resources for puzzle enthusiasts in one accessible place.

**🎮 [WordScramble.eu](https://wordscramble.eu)**

A collection of browser-based word games, including Scrabble-style challenges and word scramble puzzles. Designed for players who enjoy testing their vocabulary, discovering new words and having fun with classic word games.
